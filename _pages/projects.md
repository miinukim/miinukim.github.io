## Research Projects

### Quantum-Neural Hybrid Variational Algorithms

**2024-Present**

- Developed and analyzed diagonal neural post-processing methods for variational quantum eigensolvers.
- Studied expressivity, stability, finite-shot limitations, and a unitary variant for shot-based estimation.
- Implemented numerical experiments with Qiskit and PyTorch.

### Quantum Reservoir Computing

**2026-Present**

- Analyzed measurement and readout constraints on accessible latent spaces in quantum reservoir models.
- Developed expressivity criteria and simulation tools for reservoir dynamics and visible operator subspaces.

### Trapped-Ion Quantum-Computer Architecture and Software

**2025-Present**

- Designed a modular architecture covering compilation, scheduling, hardware dispatch, and result processing.
- Developed a Qiskit-compatible client for submitting circuits to laboratory hardware and retrieving results.
- Contributed to experimental control software used in the QuIQCL laboratory.
