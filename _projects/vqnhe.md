title: VQNHELib

**VQNHELib** is software for numerical experiments on diagonal neural post-processing for variational quantum eigensolvers. It supports work on expressivity, stability, finite-shot limitations, and unitary variants for shot-based estimation. The related work is *A Rigorous Hybridization of Variational Quantum Eigensolver with Classical Neural Network*.

## Scope

- Qiskit-based quantum workflow integration
- PyTorch-based classical post-processing modules
- Research code for hybrid eigensolver experiments

## Related Work

- Publication: *A Rigorous Hybridization of Variational Quantum Eigensolver with Classical Neural Network* (arXiv:2602.17295, 2026)
- Repository: [github.com/miinukim/vqnhelib](https://github.com/miinukim/vqnhelib)
